# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I reschedule a meeting?
Tell the assistant the meeting name and the new time, e.g. "move the design review to Wednesday 2pm". The assistant will check availability and update the event.

## Who can I send emails to?
The assistant can email any teammate listed in the contacts section of personal_notes.md, or any address you explicitly provide in your request. It will not invent recipients.

## Can the assistant send an email on its own?
No. By policy the assistant always drafts the email and pauses for your approval. Nothing is sent until you approve it.

## What time zone does the assistant use?
It uses your local working-hours time zone as defined in the operating policy. All calendar times are interpreted in that zone.

## How long are meetings by default?
30 minutes, unless the request implies a longer session (review, workshop, deep dive), in which case 60 minutes.

## What happens on Fridays and Saturdays?
Those days are protected. The assistant will not schedule anything then unless you mark the request as urgent.
